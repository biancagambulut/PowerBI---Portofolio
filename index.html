📊 Power BI – Population Aged 0–3 Across Europe

🏠 Home

This Power BI project explores demographic dynamics across European countries, focusing on the population aged 0–3. Early childhood data is a critical indicator for regional development, social policy planning and future education demand. Using Eurostat data and Power BI, this dashboard transforms raw demographic figures into actionable insights and comparisons.

1. Continental Overview

The first report illustrates the total 0–3 age population across the continent. It highlights the demographic size of each country and shows the distribution of early childhood population. This view helps identify the largest and smallest population groups, as well as overall demographic patterns at a continental level.

2. Country Comparison

The second report allows a comparative analysis between European countries. Users can select a specific country and compare it to European averages. This visualization supports demographic evaluation, regional inequalities and helps identify where population decline or growth is most significant.

3. Gender Analysis

The third dashboard breaks down the population by gender for each country. This analysis reveals gender differences, trends and demographic balance. Understanding these patterns is essential for social development programs and long-term planning for families and education systems.

4. Key Insights

The final section summarizes the key demographic insights from the entire analysis. It highlights countries with the highest and lowest population in the selected group, patterns by gender, and overall European trends. These findings support policy, healthcare and education planning decisions at both national and continental levels.
